## Summary

Implemented ADR-0008 and P0-S2 AnswerBridge for approvals and questions. Phones and the PC GUI can both answer pending items; the first valid answer wins, the loser is withdrawn, and being away from the PC never fails a request early. Implemented pending item registration, single-use enforcement, delta fan-out, race resolution, biometric P-256 signature verification, and root Cordis waterfall listeners.

## Changed paths

- `packages/crypto/src/approval.ts` (added `generateApprovalKeypair` and `signApprovalMessage` utilities)
- `packages/host/src/interaction/pending.ts` (`PendingRegistry`: add, get, list, resolve, cancel, subscribe)
- `packages/host/src/interaction/race.ts` (`raceApproval`, `raceQuestion`: phone vs PC GUI vs timeout vs signal, withdrawal per ADR-0008)
- `packages/host/src/interaction/dsh-waterfalls.ts` (root Cordis `prepend: true` listeners for `approval/request` and `user-questions/request`, startup self-check)
