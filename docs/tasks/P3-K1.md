## Goal

Let the owner answer approvals and questions safely from the phone: clear previews, biometric-bound signatures for high-risk approvals, an approvals inbox, plan reviews, and the app lock.

## Inputs

RCP/1 §8, Crypto/1 §7, ADR-0007, blueprint §10.4–§10.6, §11.4–§11.5, `docs/spikes/P0-S5.md`.

## Deliverables

- Approval takeover in the conversation composer and an Approvals inbox across hosts: tool, command/args preview (monospace, horizontally scrollable, never truncated silently), reason, risk badge, expiry countdown; Approve / Reject.
- High risk (or `requiresSignature`): recompute `argsDigest` from displayed bytes (refuse on mismatch), BiometricPrompt with `CryptoObject(Signature)`, send `sig`; handle `signature_*` errors.
- Question takeover: options, multi-select, free text, plan-review presentation (render `detail` markdown; Approve maps to `intent.approve`).
- App lock (cold start + 5 min background), `FLAG_SECURE` on sensitive screens (setting), approval-key rotation flow after biometric enrollment change (`devices.rotateApprovalKey` → wait for PC confirmation).
- Resolution handling: `resolved{by:pc}` removes the card with a snackbar.

## Acceptance

- [ ] High-risk approval impossible without a fingerprint on the owner's phone; normal-risk approvals need only the unlocked app.
- [ ] Digest mismatch test (tampered preview in a fake repository) blocks signing.
- [ ] UI tests for approval, question, plan review, resolved-elsewhere.

## Constraints

- No approve/reject actions from notifications.

## Stop and report

- The owner's device cannot do per-use biometric keys (switch to the ADR-0007 credential fallback only with owner approval).
