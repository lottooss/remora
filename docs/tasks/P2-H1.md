## Goal

Implement pairing exactly as [Crypto/1 §5](../specs/crypto-v1.md#5-pairing) and blueprint §11.1 describe, the persistent device registry, and the PC-side management page (pair, confirm SAS, list, revoke, status).

## Inputs

Crypto/1 §5, §10; RCP/1 §7; blueprint §8.9, §11.1, §11.8; `docs/spikes/P0-S1.md` (Fetch routes, credentials, storage domain).

## Deliverables

- `PairingService`: begin (ticket from relay, pairing secret, QR payload, 10-min expiry), pairing-channel handling (msg1 verification rules), SAS computation, confirm/reject/timeout, `pair.complete` request with persistence-before-ack, relay `endpoint.revoke` on failure.
- `DeviceRegistry` over `ctx.storage.domain` (public data) + `ctx.credentials` (devicePsk, pushKey); revoke closes live channels.
- Management page under `/api/remora/`: HTML (no external assets), QR as inline SVG (`qrcode`), SAS confirm/reject (POST, same-origin), device list with revoke, status panel. Terminal QR printed when stdout is a TTY and no device is paired.
- Tests: full pairing with the testkit device (confirm, reject, timeout, ticket reuse, wrong PSK, device-id mismatch), revoke during an active channel.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Pairing cannot complete without a POST confirmation carrying the displayed SAS id.
- [ ] Management page requires the dsh browser cookie; a cross-origin POST is rejected.
- [ ] After revoke, the device's next handshake is dropped and the relay reports it revoked.

## Constraints

- The page reveals no secrets; the QR is shown only while a pairing attempt is open and disappears after use.

## Stop and report

- Fetch routes cannot inherit dsh authentication (fall back to terminal-only pairing and ask the Integrator).

## Verify

```sh
pnpm -F @remora/host test && pnpm test:e2e -t pairing
```
